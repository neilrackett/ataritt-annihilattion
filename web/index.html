AnnihilaTTion

Welcome to the data converter for AnnihilaTTion, the Total Annihilation engine for the Atari TT.

Total Annihilation is still copyright of its owners, so AnnihilaTTion comes without its data: it plays the free demo's own, converted for the TT from the demo's installer.

Download Total Annihilation.exe, the demo's installer, from the Internet Archive and drop it below. You'll get an ANNIHILA folder with the files in its DATA folder. ANNIHILA.TOS goes in the ANNIHILA folder, beside DATA: download it from neilrackett.com.

The conversion runs the same Python as a build on a Mac or Linux, and makes the same files. The first time, it downloads about 10MB of Python, and the conversion itself takes a few minutes.

Everything happens in this browser. The installer is never uploaded.

Drop Total Annihilation.exe here
